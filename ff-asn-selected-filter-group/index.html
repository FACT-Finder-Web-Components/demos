This demo creates a dummy ASN group out of all selected filters and puts it as the first group of ff-asn.

Its final functionality is similar to ff-filter-cloud.

Taking this approach instead of using the built-in component can be considered when implementing designs where the selected filters should be rendered as a part of ASN to simplify styling
